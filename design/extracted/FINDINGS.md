## The correction this forces

**The real Saharna does not look like the Figma comp, and it does not look like
what I generated.**

| | Figma comp / my plates | The actual monastery |
|---|---|---|
| Walls | whitewashed lime, chalk white | **warm yellow ochre** with white pilaster trim |
| Roofs | dark grey wood shingle | **green sheet metal**, some **cobalt blue** |
| Domes | pale, modest | **green/teal**, gold crosses |
| Setting | bare, misty, sober | **dense green wooded limestone gorge** |
| Accent | gold | **blue** — blue roofs, blue window frames, blue fences, blue cemetery crosses |

Every P1 and P2 plate I generated is architecturally wrong. I described a generic
Moldovan church from the comp instead of reading the place first. The parchment/sand/gold
palette is inherited from the polished Figma comp — it is not measured off Saharna.

## The interiors are the real find

The `church-sfanta-treime` set is not scenery, it is source material:

- **The actual iconostasis of Saharna**, photographed straight on — five tiers, real
  panel proportions. The grid thesis now has a measurable original instead of a theory.
- **Oklad and riza metalwork** — repoussé silver, enamel, filigree. Real ornament to
  draw from, better than any generated Byzantine border.
- **The dome Pantocrator** and framing roundels.
- **Frescoes with Romanian Cyrillic lettering** — actual archaic letterforms in situ.
  Better type reference than the P5 specimens I was going to generate.

## What this means for the build

1. **Stop generating architecture.** There are 701 real photographs of this monastery.
2. Credits should go to what no photograph provides: **orthographic elevations for the
   3D pass**, and only after the plates are corrected to ochre walls and green roofs.
3. **Ornament comes from the oklads**, traced — not from prompts.
4. **Type reference comes from the frescoes**, not from generated specimens.
5. The palette needs rebuilding around **green/blue exterior, ochre/gold interior**.
